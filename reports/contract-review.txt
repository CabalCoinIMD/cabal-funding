CABAL FUNDING CONTRACT AND FACTORY REVIEW
18 October 2026

Outcome
The 4/4 custom fee and 70/30 funding design passed this review's tests. The
deployed Robinhood IMD LaunchFactory path was verified on a disposable local fork,
including the actual IMD token, PoolManager, LaunchFees and Intake runtimes. No
public launch, publication, paid request or real expenditure occurred.

This is an implementation review by the coding agent, not an independent audit,
an IMD approval, or a guarantee that no vulnerability remains.

Findings fixed
M1 - Stale permissionless maintenance could block paid request processing.
Location: worker/engine.mjs; worker/store.mjs.
Anyone can call harvest/payTreasury. If another caller settled some or all of a
saved unsigned maintenance operation, its next gas estimation reverted. The worker
retried the same stale amount indefinitely and could hold requests behind it.
The worker now resizes only unsigned maintenance to the available balance, or
marks it superseded if nothing remains. Signed transactions are never rewritten.
Regression tests cover complete and partial external settlement, including the
immutability of already-signed bytes. Severity: medium availability issue.

M2 - Admission could use a stale funding balance during payment reconciliation.
Location: worker/budget.mjs.
Admission read the chain asynchronously before reserving funds. A simultaneous
worker could finalize a payment and reduce the database reservation between the
balance read and new admission, counting already-spent funds as available.
Admission now holds and checks the same coordinator lease across its balance read
and reservation. Concurrent workers cannot reconcile during that window; expired
leases refuse the reservation. Two regression tests cover both cases.
Severity: medium reservation/availability issue; not an on-chain withdrawal exploit.

Launch preparation gap addressed
The component package had the hook and mock tokens but no production launch token.
Added LaunchToken: Cabal Coin / CABAL, exactly 1 billion, 18 decimals, no constructor
arguments, no privileged minting and plain transfers. It mints to the deploying
factory so the factory can perform the supply allocation. This remains local code.

Contract review coverage
- Tax bases and exact-input/output deltas on buys and sells, both token orderings.
- Claims rather than immediate withdrawal during swaps, including token-only seed.
- Cumulative rounding, reserve solvency, IMD claim backing and separate treasury.
- Permissionless settlement with fixed destinations; failure rollback and reentrancy.
- Factory-only first initialization; fixed bound pool; manager-only callbacks.
- Immutable tax/split/intake/treasury, explicit owner and worker authority boundaries.
- Exact intake approval/debit, duplicate decision keys, limits, expiry and daily reset.
- Partial-fill atomic rollback, prefunding and direct-donation accounting.
- Constructor shape, hook CREATE2 permissions and deployed runtime size.
No additional reproducible high/critical contract defect was identified in this
scope. This does not clear all dependency or economic risks.

Residual launch requirements and limits
1. IMD's off-chain build, manifest and independent review have not run for this
   source. Use launch kind univ4_hook, not an evm_project launch with its default
   guard. Manifest arguments: $poolManager, $factory, Robinhood IMD, Robinhood
   Intake, $owner, and the explicitly designated treasury. No launch.json was made.
2. Owner, treasury and supply remainder roles are assigned in the separate launch
   draft. Worker, caps and minimum reserve remain unset. Do not fund a predicted
   address before the approved manifest and deployment are checked.
3. The original factory test used a Uniswap test router. The subsequent review
   exercises the deployed production router/quoter locally. Recheck current
   deployments, frontend encoding and the final pool before trading opens. Pin
   the reviewed source repository/commit; IMD adaptation can change tested code.
4. Request payments need the game coordinator, durable database, signer injection,
   IMD result verification and timeout behavior. Those are not enabled here.
5. The worker is trusted to ask legitimate questions within caps. A compromised
   worker can waste its allowed daily request budget. The owner can raise caps;
   limits therefore do not constrain a compromised owner. UTC-day limits allow
   spending on both sides of midnight, rather than imposing a rolling 24-hour cap.
6. The treasury and Intake are immutable, and there is no arbitrary owner sweep.
   A bad treasury address, lost owner key, or retired Intake can strand funds or
   administration. Do not prefund large sums until addresses and lifecycle are set.
7. This review assumes the deployed IMD token and PoolManager remain suitable;
   no fee-on-transfer/rebasing token or arbitrary pool pairing is supported.
8. Trading volume does not guarantee sufficient IMD revenue, nor does the tax fund
   the worker's native ETH gas. Funding/maintenance require a running coordinator.
9. Windows Foundry Solar preprocessing emits import-resolution diagnostics; solc
   compilation and EVM tests pass. No Slither/static-analysis clearance is claimed.

Safety and privacy
The fork's public RPC proxy allows only read methods. Writes went exclusively to
local chain 31337; the deployer was impersonated locally and fixture IMD was added
through local storage. No real wallet key, project account or branded launch body
was submitted to IMD. Live execution remains disabled.
